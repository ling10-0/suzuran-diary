# Opening story asset

The story onboarding in `public/onboarding.js` / `public/onboarding.css` looks for this optional image:

- `public/assets/story/cityhall-story-bg.png`

If the image is absent, the intro still renders with the built-in sepia gradient fallback. Once the image is uploaded with this exact filename, it is used automatically as the animated scene background.

Recommended export: 16:9 or wider, around 1536×1024 or 1600×900. Keep the young bespectacled female city-hall official on the left and the trainee reporter on the right, leaving the lower area relatively quiet because the website draws its own dialogue box over the scene.
